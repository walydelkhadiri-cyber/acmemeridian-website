Asked first.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Companies of any size that need software done properly — from a first website for a small business to a SaaS product or an internal system for an established team. We work remotely with clients worldwide.

Can you take over what we already have?

Yes. We start by reviewing what exists — code, hosting, accounts — then maintain it, improve it step by step, or recommend a rebuild when that is genuinely cheaper over time.

Do you also run things after launch?

Yes. Alongside new builds we offer website management and social media management, so the team that built your product keeps it current and visible.

How is a project priced?

After a short discovery we send a written proposal with the scope, the timeline and the price. Nothing starts until it is agreed in writing.

Who will I be talking to?

The person building your project. No sales layer, no ticket queue — one reply, usually within a day.
