A meridian is a fixed line in a moving world.

Most software projects fail on coordination, not code — a designer here, an agency there, a freelancer who disappears. We keep the whole line in one hand: strategy, design, engineering, deployment and the years after.

Small team. Senior work. Direct access to the person writing your code.
